# DevMock Interest Validation Strategy

## Posting Schedule & Platforms

### Week 1: Initial Launch
- **Day 1**: Dev.to blog post (primary content piece)
- **Day 2**: r/webdev Reddit post (technical audience)
- **Day 4**: r/SideProject Reddit post (entrepreneurial angle)
- **Day 7**: Follow-up engagement on all platforms

### Week 2: Expand Reach
- **Twitter/X**: Thread summarizing key points
- **LinkedIn**: Professional development angle
- **Hacker News**: Submit Dev.to article (if it gains traction)
- **Discord communities**: Share in relevant developer servers

## Key Metrics to Track

### Primary Metrics (Business Validation)
- **Waitlist signups**: Target 100+ in first 2 weeks
- **Email click-through rates**: Measure engagement quality
- **Conversion from post views to waitlist**: Aim for 2-5%

### Secondary Metrics (Community Engagement)
- **Dev.to**: Views, hearts, comments, follows
- **Reddit**: Upvotes, comments, engagement quality
- **Overall reach**: Total views across all platforms

### Qualitative Feedback Tracking
- **Pain points mentioned**: What problems do people actually face?
- **Feature requests**: What would make people switch from current tools?
- **Pricing sensitivity**: Reactions to different pricing models
- **Use cases**: How would people actually use DevMock?

## Success Criteria

### Minimum Viable Interest
- **50+ waitlist signups** from organic posts
- **10+ detailed comments** with feature requests/feedback
- **5+ developers** expressing willingness to pay

### Strong Interest Indicators
- **100+ waitlist signups** 
- **Multiple feature requests** for same functionality
- **Developers sharing** their current mock API setups
- **Questions about beta access** or early launch

### Product-Market Fit Signals
- **People sharing the posts** without prompting
- **"I would pay for this"** type comments
- **Detailed use case discussions** in comments
- **Requests for integration** with specific tools

## Engagement Best Practices

### Responding to Comments
- **Response time**: Within 4-6 hours during business hours
- **Tone**: Helpful, curious, not salesy
- **Ask follow-up questions**: Dig deeper into use cases
- **Acknowledge pain points**: Show you understand their problems

### Content Adaptation
- **Track what resonates**: Which examples get most engagement?
- **Iterate messaging**: Refine value proposition based on feedback
- **Address concerns**: Common objections should be addressed in future posts

## Follow-up Strategy

### Week 2 Actions
- **Update posts** with "Edit: Thanks for feedback, here's what I'm hearing..."
- **Create follow-up content** addressing common questions
- **Reach out individually** to highly engaged commenters

### Month 1 Follow-up
- **Dev.to follow-up post**: "What I learned from validating DevMock"
- **Email to waitlist**: Share development progress and key insights
- **Community building**: Start Discord/Slack for interested developers

## Red Flags to Watch For

### Low Interest Signals
- **<20 waitlist signups** after 2 weeks
- **Generic positive comments** without specific use cases
- **No feature requests** or technical questions
- **High bounce rate** from posts to landing page

### Pivot Indicators
- **"Just use JSON Server"** repeated feedback
- **Price sensitivity** concerns dominate discussion
- **Limited use cases** mentioned by community
- **Existing solutions** meet 90% of needs

## Data Collection Setup

### Analytics Tracking
- **Google Analytics** on landing page
- **UTM parameters** for each post platform
- **Waitlist signup source** tracking
- **Time on page** and bounce rate monitoring

### Feedback Organization
- **Notion database** for all comments and feedback
- **Tag system**: Feature requests, pain points, pricing, use cases
- **Weekly review** of collected feedback
- **Share insights** with potential future team members

## Budget Considerations

### Free Promotion (Week 1-2)
- **Organic posting**: Dev.to, Reddit, personal social media
- **Community engagement**: Discord servers, Slack channels
- **Email outreach**: Personal network for feedback

### Paid Options (If organic shows promise)
- **Reddit promoted posts**: $50-100 budget
- **Twitter/X promotion**: $25-50 budget  
- **Dev.to newsletter**: Consider if available

## Success Follow-up Plan

### If Validation Succeeds
1. **Beta development**: Start building based on feedback
2. **Community building**: Create developer community
3. **Content strategy**: Regular updates on development
4. **Early access program**: Reward early supporters

### If Validation Fails
1. **Pivot analysis**: What problems did people actually mention?
2. **Feature reduction**: Could a simpler version work?
3. **Market research**: Are there adjacent problems to solve?
4. **Learning documentation**: Share the validation process
